## How it works
Turning the handle (A1006) rotates the pinion shaft (A1003). The pinion's teeth mesh with the rack (A1002), driving it down toward the table (A1005). The faceplate (A1004) and two gib strips (A1009) hold the rack in its slot in the frame (A1001), and set screws with lock nuts press on the gibs to take up any play.
