## How it works
Turning the lever arm (A1006) rotates the pinion shaft (A1003). Its teeth mesh with the rack teeth on the ram (A1002), driving the ram down toward the press plate (A1005). The face plate (A1004) and two gibb plates (A1009) hold the ram in its slot in the frame (A1001), and set screws with jam nuts press on the gibb plates to take up any play.
