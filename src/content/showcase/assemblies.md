I built the A1000 Arbor Press by inserting the Frame as a *Fixed* component and bringing in the other parts as *Floating* components. *Coincident*, *Concentric*, and *Distance* mates locate each part, and a *Rack and Pinion* mechanical mate links the pinion's rotation to the rack's travel, so turning the handle drives the press. The bolts, set screws, and nuts are purchased McMaster-Carr parts, and the *Exploded View* with *Explode Lines* (use the Explode button on the model) shows how every piece goes together.
