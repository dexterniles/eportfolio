The oven rack is wire bent in three dimensions, so I drew its path as a *3D Sketch* with *Sketch Fillets* at the bends and turned it into wire with a *Swept Boss/Base*, then repeated the cross wires with a *Linear Pattern*. For the light switch cover I *Researched* a standard single-toggle plate on *McMaster-Carr* and used its dimensions as my *Reference*, so it would fit a real switch and wall box. I modeled the plate with an *Extruded Boss/Base* and cut the toggle slot and screw holes with *Extruded Cuts* at the catalog's locations. To make it my own, I cut a sunburst around the top screw and a tapered groove down to the bottom screw into the face as a *Decorative Feature*.
