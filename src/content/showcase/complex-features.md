On the Pressure Plate I modeled one hollow boss, repeated it around the center axis with a *Circular Pattern*, and added an R2 *Fillet* where each boss meets the plate. The same idea made the four slots in the A1005 Press Plate and the teeth on the A1003 Pinion Shaft, while a *Linear Pattern* spaced the teeth along the A1002 Ram. The SolidWorks tutorial block taught me the *Shell* feature, which hollows a solid to an even wall thickness in one step. The brackets and the CAD111-0004 Arm needed *Hole Wizard* holes, a *Straight Slot*, and careful choice of which sketch became the *Base Feature*.
