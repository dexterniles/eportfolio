The parts in this group needed features that build on a basic extrude. On the Pressure Plate I modeled one hollow boss, repeated it around the center axis with a *Circular Pattern*, and added an R2 *Fillet* where each boss meets the plate, while the A1005 Slotted Disc used the same *Circular Pattern* for its four slots. The SolidWorks tutorial block taught me the *Shell* feature, which hollows a solid to an even wall thickness in one step. The brackets and the CAD111-0004 Crank Arm needed *Hole Wizard* holes, a *Straight Slot*, and careful choice of which sketch became the *Base Feature*.
