Every part in this group began as a *Sketch* on the *Front Plane*, *Top Plane*, or *Right Plane*, started at the *Origin* and built from *Lines*, *Circles*, and *Arcs*. I used *Smart Dimension* and *Relations* such as *Horizontal*, *Coincident*, and *Concentric* until each sketch was *Fully Defined*, with *Trim Entities* to clean up overlapping geometry. Flat parts like the CAD111 blocks and the V1003 Jaw Plate came from an *Extruded Boss/Base* with *Extruded Cuts* set to *Through All*, while round parts like the pins and the V1008 Drilled Ball were a *Revolved Boss/Base* around a *Centerline*. Small details such as the *Chamfer* on each end of the C1004 Pin finished the parts.
